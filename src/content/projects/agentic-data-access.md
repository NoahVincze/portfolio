Data democratization means that the people with a question can get a trustworthy answer from the data themselves, without queueing for a specialist. My work in Bayer's ML & AI unit is about making that possible with AI agents: agentic enablement, so that asking a question of the data becomes as direct as asking a colleague.

## Approach

Most of this work is internal, so this page stays at the level of the problem rather than the systems. If you would like to hear more about how we approach it, get in touch.
